Privacy Policy for iBudgetBuddy

Last updated: January 2026

iBudgetBuddy (“the App”) is developed by VitalCode Labs (“we”, “us”, or “our”). Your privacy is important, and this policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

iBudgetBuddy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All budgeting information — including transactions, categories, and preferences — is stored locally on your device. None of this data is sent to our servers or any third parties.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Because the App does not collect or transmit data:

  • No personal information is used for analytics
  • No information is used for advertising
  • No information is shared with third parties

All calculations and storage occur entirely on your device.

3. Third‑Party Services

The App does not integrate with third‑party analytics, advertising networks, or external data processors. If you choose to use optional features such as iCloud backup (if enabled in a future update), Apple’s own privacy policies apply to that service.

4. Children’s Privacy

iBudgetBuddy does not collect any personal information and is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

5. Security

All data remains on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any backups you create.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements or new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
